# Video recipes (2D Studio)

Each recipe is a skill measured from a reference short: script structure, pacing, camera, places, light, characters.
Pick the one whose **premise pattern** fits the user's topic (not the one whose original topic sounds similar). If two
fit, prefer the one not used in the last videos (`Library/plans/*.json`, field `skill`). Then read
`video-studio` and the chosen recipe and make the plan.

| skill | format | premise pattern | fits topics like |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/v01-rule-break-why` | Giải thích "Vì sao…" | An everyday act in an extreme place has a hidden, disastrous side effect; a newcomer ignores the rule, the consequence looks like a mystery, then the physics behind the rule is explained | Strict rules in odd places whose reason is hidden: why divers must not hold their breath going up, why pilots dump fuel before landing, why submarines ban whistling, why you cannot light a match on a gas tanker, why mountaineers do not shout on certain slopes |
| `/v02-paradox-twist-story` | paradox twist story | Someone does a thing that seems pointless or self-defeating; the story piles up why it cannot work, then a hidden arrangement between unexpected partners reveals why it works perfectly | Stories where a seemingly pointless act hides a clever deal: a thief who returns what he steals, a worker who pays to go to work, a shop that sells below cost, a smuggler who declares everything, a gambler who always bets to lose |
| `/v03-dud-delayed-payoff` | Cách nó từng hoạt động | An underdog's invention is dismissed as a failure, the enemy treats it as harmless and plays with it, then the hidden mechanism inside reveals it was working all along | Clever historical devices or tricks that only work after a delay: a time-lock safe, a slow-acting poison test, a trick lock that seals itself, a decoy that waits for the enemy to act, a trap that springs when opened |
